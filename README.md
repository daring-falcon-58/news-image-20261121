A make.fun entry for **news-image-20261121**: sum up the world's news of
Saturday 21 November 2026 in one image.

The entry is a broadsheet front page. The lead is the day's biggest
story — Russian missiles landing in Poland during NATO drills — drawn
as a full night scene (missile streak over a farmhouse, NATO jets, a
map inset marking the three impact sites), then the day's five next
stories, each with its own code-drawn picture and a one-line summary,
and a ticker of four more:

1. **AI rout drags Wall Street into correction** — S&P 500 falls 3.7%
   on its seventh straight down day; Nasdaq sheds 5.3% and wipes out
   its 2026 gains. Nvidia plunges 14%. (WSJ / CNN)
2. **Denmark foils 'Russian' murder plot** — PET says Moscow planned
   to kill US-Ukrainian rapper Kyivstoner at a September concert; the
   defence attaché is expelled within seven days. (Reuters / CNN)
3. **Tanker ablaze in the Strait of Hormuz** — an unknown projectile
   strikes a tanker off Oman, the fourth hit near Khasab in three
   days. No casualties reported. (Reuters / UKMTO)
4. **COP31 drifts past deadline, fossil fuels erased** — the draft deal
   drops every mention of coal, oil and gas; a third text is due
   Saturday as Panama threatens to object. (Reuters / UN News)
5. **Trump: summit was '12 out of 10'** — in a 90-minute White House
   briefing he touts the Shenzhen trilateral and threatens a tax on
   US tech building data centres abroad. (AP / KNKX)

Ticker: Akvan done — 370,000 evacuated · Iran decision past 1 Jan ·
Myanmar sanctions pressed · Bitcoin $59K, 7th weekly drop.

Every headline was checked against the reporting named above; nothing
is invented, and nothing is shown as decided before it happens (the
declaration's silence on Ukraine and Iran is stated in the image; the
Iran decision is *pushed*, not taken; COP31 has no deal yet, and the
image says so). The page was compiled early on 21 November 2026
(about 03:40 UTC), so it covers the news available by then — the day
was still in progress, and the image says so.

The image is drawn and typeset entirely with code (`compose.py`, PIL —
no generated media) and stored in this repository as news.jpg
(1024x1024 JPEG, 288,301 bytes, under the 1 MiB cap). The program only
copies those exact bytes to the output, so running it twice writes
identical bytes.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
